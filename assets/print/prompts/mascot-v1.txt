A friendly, expressive mascot character for "pashtelka.news" — a
Ukrainian-language news media for the Ukrainian community living in
Portugal. The name "pashtelka" / "pastelka" plays on the Portuguese
"pastel" (the famous pastel de nata) and a soft Ukrainian diminutive,
roughly meaning "the little colourful one".

Subject: a small, plump, friendly bird-shaped mascot, plump and rounded,
with large expressive eyes and a tiny smiling beak. The bird should feel
welcoming, gentle, slightly cheeky — a friend, not a logo.

Style: a hybrid of modern flat cartoon illustration and pixel-art accents.
Mostly clean flat vector shapes, but the wing feathers and tail have a
subtle pixel-grid texture giving it a hint of game-character charm. No
photorealism. Crisp, friendly, mobile-thumbnail-legible.

Palette: warm pastel colours. The body in soft cream (#fefcfa) with a
warm amber accent (#d97706) on the wing tips and crest, and a touch of
azulejo blue (#7ba8c7) on the eye details and on a small bow tie or
shoulder ribbon. Warm sunset palette overall — golden hour, not midday.

Composition: the mascot is centred, facing slightly forward and to the
left, holding a small pastel de nata (Portuguese custard tart) in its
wings. Behind it, very subtly, hint at Lisbon: a faint pattern of azulejo
tiles on a pale wall, a distant silhouette of the 25 de Abril bridge over
the Tagus, and a tiny yellow tram in the lower right corner. These
backdrop elements should be soft, low-contrast, never competing with the
mascot itself.

Background: simple solid pastel-cream or transparent — the mascot will be
cut out and re-placed on stickers, posters, and digital cards, so the
edge silhouette must be clean.

Output: square, 1024x1024 minimum. NO TEXT in the image.
